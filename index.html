Hong Kong vs. Norway

I think it is very interesting to experience the differences between these two countries. The culture is different in many ways, from everyday life and social habits to how people communicate and spend their time. The climate is also very different, with Hong Kong being much warmer and more humid compared to Norway's colder and more seasonal weather. I am also fascinated by the history of Hong Kong, especially its unique development and international identity. Comparing Hong Kong and Norway gives me a deeper understanding of how culture, environment, and history shape society.
